# Before publishing

1. Replace every occurrence of `https://YOUR-SITE.pages.dev/` with the actual Cloudflare Pages URL or your custom domain in `index.html`, `scholarships.html`, and `sitemap.xml`.
2. Replace `replace-with-your-email@example.com` in `contact.html` and `privacy.html` with the real editorial email address.
3. Review the Privacy Policy and update it if you add Google Analytics, AdSense, contact forms, newsletter tools, or other third-party services.
4. Test every navigation link on a phone.
5. Submit `https://YOUR-SITE.pages.dev/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console after the site is live.
6. Do not add advertisements until the website has substantial original content and the privacy/disclosure pages accurately describe the services in use.
