About us

Clear information for Nigerian students

Naija Student Guide is an independent information website for students and applicants in Nigeria. We publish plain-language guides about scholarships, admissions, application skills and online safety.

What we do

We explain difficult processes, link to original sources and show when information was last reviewed. We do not award scholarships, make admission decisions or represent government ministries, universities or scholarship providers.

What we do not promise

We do not guarantee that a reader will receive an award, admission or job. Requirements and deadlines can change. Readers should confirm current information with the original organisation before applying or paying.
