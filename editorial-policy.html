Editorial Policy

Our purpose

Our purpose is to help Nigerian students understand education opportunities and application processes. We write for readers first, not simply to attract search traffic.

Sources

For current opportunities, we look for the original organisation’s official website or an independently verifiable institutional source. We distinguish official announcements from our own explanations.

Dates and updates

Articles show a publication date and, where relevant, a last-reviewed date. Time-sensitive pages should be checked again before a reader applies. Closed or outdated opportunities should be labelled clearly.

Corrections

If you find an error, contact us with the article link and a reliable source. We will review the claim and correct material errors as soon as reasonably possible.

Independence

Naija Student Guide is independent. We are not a scholarship provider, government agency, university or recruitment company.
